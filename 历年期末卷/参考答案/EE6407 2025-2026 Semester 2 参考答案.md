## Question 1

### 1(a) 双骑士巡游的 GA 适应度（18 分）

**考点**：读懂陌生问题的编码/解码规则，把"解码过程"写成适应度函数并手工求值；本质是**排列编码 + 逐基因检查合法性**。

**题意**：$8\times8$ 棋盘按行编号（第 1 行 1–8，……，第 8 行 57–64）。染色体 $K=\langle a_1\dots a_{64}\rangle$ 是 1–64 的排列：前 32 个基因是白骑士依次到达的格子，后 32 个是黑骑士的。白骑士从 $a_1$ 出发，只要 $a_i\to a_{i+1}$ 是合法马步就继续，遇到第一个非法跳转即停止；黑骑士从 $a_{33}$ 开始同理。适应度 = 两个骑士征服格数之和。

**Answer**

**(i) Fitness function.** Row and column of square $a$:

$$r(a)=\lceil a/8\rceil,\qquad c(a)=((a-1)\bmod8)+1$$

Knight-move indicator:

$$v(a,b)=\begin{cases}1,&\{|r(a)-r(b)|,|c(a)-c(b)|\}=\{1,2\}\\0,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$

Squares conquered (start square counts as 1; the running product stops counting after the first invalid move):

$$L_W(K)=1+\sum_{i=1}^{31}\prod_{j=1}^{i}v(a_j,a_{j+1}),\qquad L_B(K)=1+\sum_{i=33}^{63}\prod_{j=33}^{i}v(a_j,a_{j+1})$$

$$\boxed{f(K)=L_W(K)+L_B(K)},\qquad2\le f\le64$$

The GA maximises $f$; $f=64$ means both knights complete 32 squares and cover the board. Because of the **permutation encoding**, no square repeats, so "no re-entering a conquered square" and "two knights cannot share a square" are **guaranteed by the encoding**; the fitness only checks knight-move validity. To normalise use $f/64$.

> 假设：起始格计入已征服。若只数跳转次数，则 $f'=f-2$，结果为 $4+7=11$。

**(ii) Fitness of the given chromosome.**

### 1(b) 早熟收敛与探索/开发平衡（7 分）

**Answer**

**Diagnosis:** stagnating at sub-optimal solutions after few generations is **premature convergence**, caused by **too much exploitation and too little exploration**: **selection pressure is too high**, a few "super individuals" take over, **diversity is lost**, and crossover only recombines near-identical individuals, producing no new genes, so **the search is trapped in a local optimum**.

- **Exploration:** sampling new regions (mutation, diverse population). **Exploitation:** refining around known good solutions (selection, crossover, elitism).

**Remedies (increase exploration):**

1. **Increase mutation rate / step size**, or use adaptive mutation that grows when diversity drops.
2. **Reduce selection pressure:** rank selection or small tournaments instead of fitness-proportional; sigma scaling; fewer elites.
3. **Increase population size** with a well-spread initial population.
4. **Maintain diversity explicitly:** fitness sharing, crowding (niching); island models.
5. **Inject new individuals:** random immigrants, partial restart on stagnation.
6. **More explorative crossover:** uniform crossover, BLX-$\alpha$.

**Balance:** too much exploration degenerates into random search. **Ideal: dynamic adjustment — high mutation and low selection pressure early, then lower mutation and higher selection pressure later**; local search at the end (memetic algorithm).

**【中文】** 诊断：几代后停滞在次优解是**早熟收敛**，原因是**开发过强、探索不足**——选择压力太大，少数超级个体占满种群，多样性丧失，交叉只在几乎相同的个体间重组，搜索被困在局部最优。措施：提高变异率/步长或自适应变异；降低选择压力（排序选择、小规模锦标赛、适应度缩放）；增大种群；显式维持多样性（fitness sharing、crowding、岛屿模型）；注入新个体；换探索性更强的交叉（均匀交叉、BLX-$\alpha$）。平衡：探索过度会退化为随机搜索，理想是**动态调节**——前期高变异低选择压力，后期反之，后期可加局部搜索。

**易错点**：只罗列措施而不从 exploration/exploitation 角度解释原因。

### 2(a) LDA 分类器设计与适用条件（12 分）

**考点**：$\mathbf w=\mathbf S_W^{-1}(\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2)$，$w_0=-\frac12\mathbf w^T(\mathbf m_1+\mathbf m_2)$；LDA 与 Bayes 最优分类器的关系。

**Answer**

**(i) Design**

$$\mathbf S_W=\begin{bmatrix}228.9365&10.9882\\10.9882&189.1215\end{bmatrix},\quad\det\mathbf S_W=228.9365\times189.1215-10.9882^2=43176.07$$

$$\mathbf S_W^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}0.004380&-0.000254\\-0.000254&0.005302\end{bmatrix},\quad\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2=\begin{bmatrix}-1.2862\\1.5321\end{bmatrix}\Rightarrow\mathbf w=\begin{bmatrix}-0.006024\\0.008451\end{bmatrix}$$

$$\mathbf m_1+\mathbf m_2=\begin{bmatrix}3.5028\\-0.6627\end{bmatrix},\quad w_0=-\tfrac12[(-0.006024)(3.5028)+0.008451(-0.6627)]=0.01335$$

$$g(\mathbf x)=-0.006024x_1+0.008451x_2+0.01335;\quad g>0\Rightarrow\omega_1,\ g<0\Rightarrow\omega_2$$

Both classes have 100 samples (equal priors), so the threshold is the midpoint of the projected means.

**(ii) Conditions under which LDA performs best**

1. **Gaussian classes with equal covariance ($\Sigma_1=\Sigma_2$).** Then **the Bayes-optimal boundary is linear, LDA coincides with the Bayes classifier and attains the minimum error.**
2. **Classes differ mainly in their means** (if the means are close, the Fisher numerator tends to 0).
3. **Each class unimodal and roughly linearly separable** (no multimodal or non-linear boundaries such as XOR, rings).
4. **$N\gg d$**, so $\mathbf S_W$ is accurately estimated and invertible.
5. **No severe outliers** (means and scatter are outlier-sensitive).
6. Midpoint threshold implicitly assumes **equal priors**; otherwise correct $w_0$.

**【中文】** (i) 设计结果如上，$g>0$ 判 $\omega_1$，两类样本数相等，阈值取投影均值中点。(ii) LDA 表现最好的条件：**①各类服从高斯分布且协方差相同**——此时 Bayes 最优边界本身是线性的，LDA 即 Bayes 最优；②类别差异主要体现在均值上；③每类单峰、近似线性可分；④样本数远大于维数，$\mathbf S_W$ 可估计且可逆；⑤无严重离群点；⑥阈值取中点隐含先验相等，不等时应修正 $w_0$。

**易错点**：(ii) 只写"线性可分"；核心答案是"高斯 + 同协方差 ⇒ LDA 即 Bayes 最优"。

### 2(b) 分类与置信度（6 分）

### 2(c) 高维小样本下 LDA 为何失效及补救（7 分）

**Answer**

**Reasons:**

- LDA needs $\mathbf S_W^{-1}$. $\mathbf S_W$ is $d\times d$, a sum of outer products of $N$ samples, so **its rank is at most $N-c$** ($N-2$ for two classes). **When $d>N-2$, $\mathbf S_W$ is singular and not invertible.**
- **Even if $d$ is slightly below $N$, $\mathbf S_W$ is ill-conditioned:** $d(d+1)/2$ parameters from too few samples, small eigenvalues are underestimated and amplified by inversion, so **$\mathbf w$ is extremely noise-sensitive**.
- Result: **excellent training performance but poor test performance** (overfitting, curse of dimensionality / peaking).

**Remedies (state one and explain):**

- **Regularised (shrinkage) LDA: replace $\mathbf S_W$ with $\mathbf S_W+\lambda\mathbf I$** — positive definite, invertible, better conditioned; $\lambda$ by cross-validation.
- Others: **PCA to $\le N-c$ dimensions** before LDA; **feature selection**; pseudo-inverse $\mathbf S_W^+$; diagonal $\mathbf S_W$ (diagonal LDA); more samples.

**【中文】** 原因：LDA 需要 $\mathbf S_W^{-1}$，而 $\mathbf S_W$ 的秩至多为 $N-c$（两类为 $N-2$），**$d>N-2$ 时 $\mathbf S_W$ 奇异、逆不存在**；即使 $d$ 略小于 $N$，$\mathbf S_W$ 也是病态的——需估计 $d(d+1)/2$ 个参数而样本不足，小特征值被严重低估、求逆时被放大，$\mathbf w$ 对噪声极敏感；结果训练集上很好、测试集上很差（过拟合）。补救：**正则化 LDA**，用 $\mathbf S_W+\lambda\mathbf I$ 代替 $\mathbf S_W$（正定可逆、条件数改善，$\lambda$ 交叉验证）；或先 PCA 降维、特征选择、伪逆、对角 LDA、增加样本。

**易错点**：只说"会过拟合"而不指出 $\mathbf S_W$ 不可逆这一直接原因。

## Question 3

### 3(a) $\xi(i)$ 与 $C$ 的作用（7 分）

**Answer**

**$\xi(i)$ — slack variable**

- **When data are not separable, the hard constraint $d(i)[\mathbf w^T\mathbf x(i)+b]\ge1$ cannot hold for all samples; $\xi(i)\ge0$ measures how much sample $i$ violates it**, making the problem feasible.
- $\xi=0$: on or outside the margin, correct. $0<\xi\le1$: inside the margin but on the correct side. $\xi>1$: misclassified. **$\sum\xi(i)$ upper-bounds the number of training errors.**

**$C$ — penalty (regularisation) parameter**

- **Controls the trade-off between maximising the margin (small $\frac12\mathbf w^T\mathbf w$) and minimising violations (small $\sum\xi$).**
- **Large $C$:** violations heavily penalised → **narrow margin, small training error, close to hard margin, prone to overfitting**, outlier-sensitive. **Small $C$:** more violations tolerated → **wider margin, simpler model, possible underfitting**.
- **$C$ is a hyperparameter chosen by cross-validation.** **In the dual, $C$ is the upper bound of the multipliers: $0\le\alpha_i\le C$.**

**【中文】** $\xi(i)$ 是**松弛变量**：数据不可分时硬间隔约束无法对所有样本成立，$\xi(i)\ge0$ 度量样本违反约束的程度；$\xi=0$ 在间隔边界上或之外且分类正确，$0<\xi\le1$ 在间隔内但仍在正确一侧，$\xi>1$ 被错分，$\sum\xi$ 是训练错分数的上界。$C$ 是**惩罚参数**：权衡"最大化间隔"与"最小化训练违例"；$C$ 大则间隔窄、训练误差小、易过拟合、对离群点敏感，$C$ 小则间隔宽、模型简单、可能欠拟合；$C$ 是超参数，用交叉验证选；对偶问题中 $0\le\alpha_i\le C$。

### 4(c) 如何确定合适的 K（5 分）

**Answer**

1. **Elbow method:** run K-means for $K=1,\dots,K_{\max}$, **compute $\text{WCSS}=\sum_{j=1}^K\sum_{\mathbf x\in D_j}\lVert\mathbf x-\mathbf m_j\rVert^2$ and plot against $K$**; WCSS drops fast before the true number of clusters and slowly after; **choose the $K$ at the elbow**.
2. **Cluster validity indices:** compute the **Silhouette coefficient** for each $K$ (or Dunn, Calinski–Harabasz: larger is better; Davies–Bouldin: smaller is better) and **choose the best $K$** — more objective when the elbow is unclear.
3. **Domain knowledge:** the application may fix the number of clusters.

**Do not simply choose the $K$ with the smallest WCSS** — it is 0 when $K=N$.

**【中文】** ①**肘部法**：对 $K=1,\dots,K_{\max}$ 分别运行 K-means，计算簇内平方和 WCSS 并画 WCSS–$K$ 曲线，取"肘部"对应的 $K$；②**聚类有效性指标**：对每个 $K$ 算 Silhouette（或 Dunn、CH 越大越好，DB 越小越好），选最优 $K$，肘部不明显时更客观；③**领域知识**：应用本身可能限定簇数。注意不能直接选 WCSS 最小的 $K$（$K=N$ 时为 0，没有意义）。

- 新增理解性问题：LDA 的最佳适用条件、高维小样本问题及补救（"为什么 / 什么时候失效 / 怎么办"是近年趋势）。
- GA 的 18 分大题是陌生场景（双骑士巡游），按题给解码规则一步步走即可，关键在细心（行列换算、第 33 个基因位置）。
- 手算 K-means 距离要保留足够位数，本卷第 1 轮两组距离只差 0.04。
