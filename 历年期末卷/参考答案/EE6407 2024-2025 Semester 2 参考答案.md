## Question 1

### 1(a) N 皇后冲突数与适应度函数（10 分）

**考点**：排列编码天然保证每行每列只有一个皇后，只需数对角线冲突；把"最小化冲突"转成 $[0,1]$ 上"最大化适应度"。

**Answer**

**(1) Number of conflicts.** Check every pair $i<j$ for "column difference = row difference" (same diagonal):

$$C(Q)=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}\sum_{j=i+1}^{n}\delta\big(|q_i-q_j|,\;j-i\big),\qquad\delta(a,b)=\begin{cases}1,&a=b\\0,&a\ne b\end{cases}$$

**(2)** For $Q=\langle2,7,8,4,6,1,3,5\rangle$ (28 pairs), the matching pairs are:

| Pair $(i,j)$ | $q_i,q_j$ | $\lvert q_i-q_j\rvert$ | $j-i$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| (1,5) | 2, 6 | 4 | 4 |
| (2,3) | 7, 8 | 1 | 1 |
| (3,5) | 8, 6 | 2 | 2 |

The other 25 pairs do not match, so **$C(Q)=3$**.

**(3) Fitness.** $n$ queens have at most $\binom n2=\frac{n(n-1)}2$ conflicting pairs; normalise and take the complement:

$$f(Q)=1-\frac{C(Q)}{n(n-1)/2}=1-\frac{2C(Q)}{n(n-1)}$$

$f=1$ when $C=0$, $f=0$ when all pairs conflict, so the range is exactly $[0,1]$. Here $f=1-3/28\approx0.893$.

> 也可以写 $f=1/(1+C)$：在 $(0,1]$ 内，无冲突为 1 但取不到 0；第一种更贴合 "range [0,1]"。

**易错点**：漏对（按 $i$ 固定、$j$ 递增系统列）；把 $\binom n2$ 写成 $n^2$ 或 $n(n-1)$；排列编码下不需要再数行/列冲突。

### 1(b) 实数编码 GA 的 flexible-α 算术交叉（15 分）

**考点**：whole arithmetic crossover $A'=\alpha A+(1-\alpha)B$，$B'=\alpha B+(1-\alpha)A$；$\alpha$ 由父代适应度决定；对算子做评价。

**Answer**

**(i) Computation.** $F(X)=\sum x_i-\sum x_i^2$.

- $A=\langle0.8,0.6,0.3,0.7,0.2\rangle$: $\sum x_i=2.6$, $\sum x_i^2=1.62$, $F(A)=0.98$
- $B=\langle0.5,0.4,0.9,0.1,1.0\rangle$: $\sum x_i=2.9$, $\sum x_i^2=2.23$, $F(B)=0.67$

$$\alpha=\frac{|F(A)-F(B)|}{F(A)+F(B)}=\frac{0.31}{1.65}=0.1879,\quad1-\alpha=0.8121$$

| Gene | $A'=\alpha A+(1-\alpha)B$ | $B'=\alpha B+(1-\alpha)A$ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.5564 | 0.7436 |
| 2 | 0.4376 | 0.5624 |
| 3 | 0.7873 | 0.4127 |
| 4 | 0.2127 | 0.5873 |
| 5 | 0.8497 | 0.3503 |

$F(A')=2.8436-1.8881=\mathbf{0.9556}$, $F(B')=2.6564-1.5073=\mathbf{1.1491}$ (maximum of $F$ is $1.25$ at all $x_i=0.5$).

**(ii) For or against (either, with reasons)**

**For:**

1. **$\alpha$ needs no manual tuning; it is set from the parents' fitness** — a **self-adaptive** mechanism.
2. **Similar parent fitness → small $\alpha$**, each child is close to the *other* parent, giving a large exchange of information between equally good solutions; a larger fitness gap gives stronger mixing.
3. $F$ is concave, so a convex combination is never worse than both parents: **$F(B')=1.149$ beats both parents**, $F(A')=0.956$ beats the weaker parent $B$.

**Against:**

1. **$\alpha$ depends only on the *size* of the fitness difference, not its *direction*:** no bias towards the fitter parent; $A'$ and $B'$ are symmetric.
2. **When $F(A)\approx F(B)$, $\alpha\to0$, so $A'\approx B$, $B'\approx A$:** crossover just swaps parents and creates no new genes, **ineffective once the population converges**.
3. **It requires $F(A)+F(B)>0$.** Here $F$ can be negative (e.g. $x_i>1$), so the denominator can be zero or negative and $\alpha$ may leave $[0,1]$ or be undefined.
4. **Offspring always lie on the segment between the parents**, so it **cannot explore beyond the parents' range**; diversity only shrinks and mutation is still needed.

> 答题建议：选一方为主，用一句话承认另一方的局限，如 "I argue for it because …; however it requires positive fitness and must be combined with mutation to keep diversity."

**【中文】** **赞成：**①$\alpha$ 由父代适应度自动确定，不需调参，是自适应机制；②父代适应度接近时 $\alpha$ 小，后代靠近对方父代，在同样好的解之间大幅交换信息；③本例 $F$ 是凹函数，后代 $B'$ 优于两个父代，$A'$ 优于较差父代。**反对：**①$\alpha$ 只取决于适应度差的大小、不含方向，不偏向更优父代；②$F(A)\approx F(B)$ 时 $\alpha\to0$，交叉退化为交换父代，种群收敛后失效；③要求 $F(A)+F(B)>0$，$F$ 可为负时 $\alpha$ 无定义或越界；④后代永远在两父代连线内，不能探索父代范围之外，仍需变异。

**易错点**：把 $\alpha$ 算成 $|F(A)-F(B)|/F(A)$；只算一个后代；(ii) 只下结论不给理由。

## Question 2

### 2(a) 不可分情形下线性 SVM 的原问题（10 分）

**考点**：soft-margin SVM primal；目标函数两项的含义；松弛变量 $\xi_i$ 与惩罚系数 $C$。

**Answer**

Given $\{\mathbf x(i),d(i)\}_{i=1}^N$, $d(i)\in\{+1,-1\}$, find $\mathbf w,b$ and slack variables $\xi(i)$ that

$$\min_{\mathbf w,b,\xi}\ \tfrac12\mathbf w^T\mathbf w+C\sum_{i=1}^N\xi(i)\quad\text{s.t.}\quad d(i)[\mathbf w^T\mathbf x(i)+b]\ge1-\xi(i),\ \ \xi(i)\ge0$$

**Rationale:**

- **$\frac12\mathbf w^T\mathbf w$:** **the margin equals $2/\lVert\mathbf w\rVert$**, so minimising $\lVert\mathbf w\rVert^2$ maximises the margin; **larger margin → better generalisation**.
- **$\sum\xi(i)$:** samples are not separable, so the hard constraint $d(i)g(\mathbf x(i))\ge1$ has no solution; **slack variables allow individual violations**. $0<\xi\le1$: inside the margin but correct; $\xi>1$: misclassified. **$\sum\xi$ upper-bounds the training errors.**
- **$C$: trades off wide margin against few violations.** **Large $C$** → narrow margin, **risk of overfitting**; **small $C$** → more violations tolerated, smoother boundary.
- **$\xi(i)\ge0$** prevents negative slack from "rewarding" well-classified samples.
- **A convex quadratic programme with a unique global optimum**, usually solved via the dual.

**【中文】** 原问题如上。$\frac12\mathbf w^T\mathbf w$：间隔为 $2/\lVert\mathbf w\rVert$，最小化它即最大化间隔、泛化更好；$\sum\xi$：样本不可分时允许个别样本违反间隔，$0<\xi\le1$ 在间隔内但分对，$\xi>1$ 被错分，$\sum\xi$ 是训练误差上界；$C$ 权衡间隔宽度与违例数，$C$ 大则间隔窄、易过拟合，$C$ 小则容忍更多违例；约束 $\xi\ge0$ 防止负松弛"奖励"；凸二次规划，全局最优唯一。

**易错点**：只写可分情形（漏 $\xi$、$C$）；不解释为什么最小化 $\lVert\mathbf w\rVert$ 等价于最大化间隔。

## Question 3

### 3(a) 朴素贝叶斯分类（10 分）

**考点**：Bernoulli NB——先验 × 条件概率连乘；比较后验（$p(\mathbf x)$ 可省）。

**Answer**

Test sample $\mathbf x=(X_1=1,X_2=1,X_3=0)$. Priors $P(\omega_1)=3/5$, $P(\omega_2)=2/5$.

| Class-conditional | $\omega_1$ (3 samples) | $\omega_2$ (2 samples) |
|---|---|---|
| $P(X_1=1\mid\omega)$ | 2/3 | 1/2 |
| $P(X_2=1\mid\omega)$ | 2/3 | 1/2 |
| $P(X_3=0\mid\omega)$ | 1/3 | 1/2 |

$$P(\mathbf x|\omega_1)P(\omega_1)=\tfrac23\cdot\tfrac23\cdot\tfrac13\cdot\tfrac35=\tfrac4{45}\approx0.0889,\qquad P(\mathbf x|\omega_2)P(\omega_2)=\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac12\cdot\tfrac25=0.05$$

$0.0889>0.05$ ⇒ **$\omega_1$** (posterior $0.64$).

**Observation:** $(1,1,0)$ appears in the training set labelled $\omega_2$, yet NB assigns $\omega_1$ — a consequence of the **conditional-independence assumption** (NB uses marginal feature statistics, not whole records).

**【中文】** 测试样本在训练集中出现过且标签为 $\omega_2$，但 NB 仍判 $\omega_1$，这是**条件独立假设**的后果——分类器用的是各特征的边缘统计而非整条记录，可作为加分点。

**易错点**：漏乘先验；$X_3=0$ 误用 $X_3=1$ 的概率；本题无零概率，不需平滑。

### 3(b) Fisher 线性判别分类器设计（10 分）

**考点**：$\mathbf S_W=\mathbf S_1+\mathbf S_2$；$\mathbf w=\mathbf S_W^{-1}(\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2)$；阈值取投影均值中点；2×2 求逆。

**Answer**

$$\mathbf S_W=\begin{bmatrix}180.9046&-27.5405\\-27.5405&176.6867\end{bmatrix},\ \det\mathbf S_W=31204.96,\ \ \mathbf S_W^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}0.005662&0.000883\\0.000883&0.005797\end{bmatrix}$$

$$\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2=\begin{bmatrix}-2.9487\\-2.6877\end{bmatrix}\Rightarrow\mathbf w=\begin{bmatrix}-0.01907\\-0.01818\end{bmatrix},\quad w_0=-\tfrac12\mathbf w^T(\mathbf m_1+\mathbf m_2)=0.04919$$

$$g(\mathbf x)=-0.01907x_1-0.01818x_2+0.04919;\quad g>0\Rightarrow\omega_1,\ g<0\Rightarrow\omega_2$$

**易错点**：把 $\mathbf S_W$ 除以样本数（课件直接用散度矩阵之和）；$\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2$ 顺序写反会使规则反号；求逆忘了交换主对角元或副对角元变号。

### 3(c) 测试样本分类与置信度（5 分）

**Answer**

$g(\mathbf x_2)=-0.01907(-0.176)-0.01818(0.224)+0.04919=0.04848>0$ ⇒ **$\omega_1$**.

$$\lVert\mathbf w\rVert=0.02635,\qquad r=\frac{\lvert g\rvert}{\lVert\mathbf w\rVert}=\frac{0.04848}{0.02635}\approx1.84$$

## Question 4

### 4(a) K-means 算法描述（5 分）

**Answer**

1. **Choose $k$ and initialise $k$ cluster centres** (e.g. randomly pick $k$ samples).
2. **Assignment:** assign each sample to the nearest centre (Euclidean distance).
3. **Update:** recompute each centre as the mean of its assigned samples.
4. **Repeat 2–3 until assignments (or centres) no longer change**, or a maximum number of iterations is reached.

It **minimises $J=\sum_{j=1}^k\sum_{\mathbf x\in D_j}\lVert\mathbf x-\mathbf m_j\rVert^2$**, only **converges to a local optimum and depends on initialisation**, so run it several times and keep the run with the smallest $J$.

**【中文】** 选 $k$ 并初始化中心 → 把样本分到最近中心 → 中心更新为簇均值 → 重复直到不再变化。目标是最小化簇内平方误差 $J$，只保证局部最优、依赖初值，故多次初始化取 $J$ 最小。

### 4(b) K 未知时如何确定 K（5 分）

**Answer**

**Elbow method:** run K-means for $k=1,2,\dots,K_{\max}$, **compute the within-cluster sum of squares (WCSS) for each $k$ and plot WCSS against $k$**. WCSS decreases monotonically, quickly at first and then **flattening once $k$ exceeds the true number of clusters**; **the $k$ at the "elbow"** (maximum curvature) is chosen. **Alternative:** compute the **Silhouette coefficient** (or Dunn index) for each $k$ and **choose the $k$ that maximises it**.

**【中文】** 肘部法：对 $k=1,\dots,K_{\max}$ 跑 K-means，画 WCSS–$k$ 曲线，取曲率最大的拐点。替代：对每个 $k$ 算 Silhouette（或 Dunn），选指标最大的 $k$。

### 4(c) 簇形状不规则时能否用 K-means（5 分）

**Answer**

**Generally not suitable.**

- K-means assigns by Euclidean distance to the mean (a Voronoi partition), which **implicitly assumes convex, roughly spherical clusters of similar size and density**.
- For non-convex shapes (moons, rings, elongated clusters) **the mean does not represent the cluster**; K-means **splits a natural cluster or merges parts of different ones**.
- It is also **sensitive to outliers and initialisation**.

**Alternatives:** **DBSCAN** (arbitrary shapes, identifies noise), single-linkage hierarchical clustering, spectral clustering; GMM for elliptical clusters.

**【中文】** 一般**不适合**。K-means 是 Voronoi 划分，隐含假设簇为凸的、近似球形、大小密度相近；对月牙形、环形、细长形等非凸簇，均值不能代表簇，会把自然簇切开或把不同簇的部分合并；还对离群点和初值敏感。替代：DBSCAN（任意形状并识别噪声）、single-linkage 层次聚类、谱聚类；椭球形簇可用 GMM。
