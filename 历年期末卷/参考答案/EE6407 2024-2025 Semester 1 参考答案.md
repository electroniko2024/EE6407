## Question 1

题目：温度模型 $f(x,y,z)=5x^2+3y^2+2z^2-7x-4y+6z$，$x,y,z\in[-10,10]$。

### 1(a) 12 位二进制编码的解码与局限（15 分）

**考点**：把二进制染色体拆成多个变量；二进制 → 十进制 → 实数区间的线性映射；二进制表示的分辨率限制。

**Answer**

**Decoding (assumption):** the 12 bits are split equally into three 4-bit genes for $x$, $y$, $z$ in that order. Each decimal value $D\in\{0,\dots,15\}$ is mapped linearly onto $[-10,10]$:

$$v=v_{\min}+\frac{v_{\max}-v_{\min}}{2^4-1}D=-10+\frac{20}{15}D=-10+\frac{4}{3}D$$

| String | Bits | $D_x,D_y,D_z$ | $(x,y,z)$ | $f(x,y,z)$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| i | 0000 0000 0000 | 0, 0, 0 | $(-10,-10,-10)$ | **1050** |
| ii | 0011 1100 1001 | 3, 12, 9 | $(-6,6,2)$ | **326** |
| iii | 1100 0111 0101 | 12, 7, 5 | $(6,-0.667,-3.333)$ | **144.22** |
| iv | 0000 0000 1111 | 0, 0, 15 | $(-10,-10,10)$ | **1170** |
| v | 1111 1111 1111 | 15, 15, 15 | $(10,10,10)$ | **950** |

Working:

- i: $500+300+200+70+40-60=1050$
- ii: $5(36)+3(36)+2(4)-7(-6)-4(6)+6(2)=180+108+8+42-24+12=326$
- iii: $5(36)+3(0.4444)+2(11.111)-42+2.667-20=144.22$
- iv: $500+300+200+70+40+60=1170$
- v: $500+300+200-70-40+60=950$

**Optimum:** all quadratic coefficients are positive, so $f$ is convex and its **maximum over the cube lies at a vertex**. Best end point per variable: $x=-10$ ($5x^2-7x=570$), $y=-10$ (340), $z=+10$ (260), so $f_{\max}=570+340+260=1170$, exactly string iv.

**Limitations of the binary representation:**

1. **Limited precision:** 4 bits per variable give only 16 values, resolution $20/15=1.333$. The GA searches only $16^3=4096$ grid points; **if the optimum is not on the grid it can only be approximated**, with error up to half a step ($\approx0.667$). E.g. 0 cannot be represented (nearest $\pm0.667$), and the minimiser $(0.7,0.667,-1.5)$ is not on the grid.
2. Here the maximum is at a vertex and **end points are always representable**, so the optimum is reachable only because of the function's shape — not in general.
3. **Cost of higher precision:** more bits, longer chromosome, search space grows as $2^L$, slower convergence.
4. **Hamming cliff:** adjacent values can differ in many bits (0111 → 1000), so a single mutation rarely reaches the neighbouring value, which **hinders fine-tuning near the optimum**; Gray coding alleviates this, and **real-valued encoding is usually more suitable** for continuous variables.

**【中文】** 解码：12 位平均分给 $x,y,z$，每个 4 位，十进制值 $D$ 线性映射到 $[-10,10]$。最优解：$f$ 是凸函数，最大值在顶点，为第 iv 串。二进制编码的局限：**①精度有限**——4 位只有 16 个值、分辨率 1.333，最优解不在格点上就只能近似，误差可达半个步长；**②本题碰巧可达**——最大值在顶点而区间端点总能被精确表示，不是一般性质；**③提高精度代价大**——位数增加、搜索空间按 $2^L$ 指数增大；**④Hamming cliff**——相邻数值二进制差很多位，妨碍最优解附近的精细搜索，可用 Gray 码，连续变量更适合实数编码。

**易错点**：分母用 $2^4=16$ 而不是 15（1111 映射不到 $+10$）；把 12 位当一个整数或按 3 位拆分；第 iii 串的 $y,z$ 要保留分数 $-2/3,-10/3$；漏答 "limitation"。

### 1(b) 用实数编码 GA 估计模型系数（10 分）

**考点**：黑盒分类中的**建模问题**——输入、输出已知求模型参数；适应度 = 预测与测量的误差（越小越好）。

**Answer**

Model: $f=2.5x^2+3.88y^2+5.6z^2-a_1x-b_1y+c_1z$, chromosome $S=\langle a_1,b_1,c_1\rangle$.

Fixed parts: point 1 $(10,5,5)$: $250+97+140=487$; point 2 $(-10,10,5)$: $250+388+140=778$. Hence

$$\hat f_1=487-10a_1-5b_1+5c_1,\qquad \hat f_2=778+10a_1-10b_1+5c_1$$

**S1 = ⟨7, 1.6, 4.8⟩:** $\hat f_1=433$, $e_1=732-433=299$; $\hat f_2=856$, $e_2=688-856=-168$.

**S2 = ⟨−5, 6.2, 3.3⟩:** $\hat f_1=522.5$, $e_1=209.5$; $\hat f_2=682.5$, $e_2=5.5$.

| Chromosome | $e_1$ | $e_2$ | $\sum\lvert e\rvert$ | $\sum e^2$ | RMSE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 299 | −168 | **467** | 117625 | 242.5 |
| S2 | 209.5 | 5.5 | **215** | 43920.5 | 148.2 |

Error is minimised, so **S2 is fitter than S1**. A "larger is better" fitness: $F=1/(1+E)$, giving 0.00214 for S1 and 0.00463 for S2.

> 题目没有规定用哪种误差，绝对误差和或平方误差和都可以；注意正负误差不能直接相加（$299+(-168)=131$ 会掩盖误差）。

补充：两个测量点只有两个方程、三个未知数，欠定，需要更多测量点。**易错点**：$a_1x,b_1y$ 前是负号，点 2 的 $x=-10$，所以 $-a_1x=+10a_1$。

## Question 2

已知：$N_1=500$，$N_2=300$；$\mathbf m_1=[0.026,0.048]^T$，$\mathbf m_2=[1.542,2.011]^T$；

$$\mathbf C_1=\begin{bmatrix}0.942&0.012\\0.012&1.087\end{bmatrix},\quad\mathbf C_2=\begin{bmatrix}0.945&0.054\\0.054&1.037\end{bmatrix}$$

### 2(a) Bayes 决策规则（12 分）

**考点**：多元高斯密度；对数判别函数；先验由样本比例估计。

**Answer**

Priors: $P(\omega_1)=500/800=0.625$, $P(\omega_2)=0.375$.

Taking logs and dropping the common constant $-\frac d2\ln2\pi$:

$$g_i(\mathbf x)=-\tfrac12(\mathbf x-\mathbf m_i)^T\mathbf C_i^{-1}(\mathbf x-\mathbf m_i)-\tfrac12\ln|\mathbf C_i|+\ln P(\omega_i)$$

Decide $\omega_1$ if $g_1>g_2$, else $\omega_2$. Since $\mathbf C_1\ne\mathbf C_2$ the boundary is quadratic.

| | $\omega_1$ | $\omega_2$ |
|---|---|---|
| $\lvert\mathbf C_i\rvert$ | $0.942(1.087)-0.012^2=1.02381$ | $0.945(1.037)-0.054^2=0.97705$ |
| $\mathbf C_i^{-1}$ | $\begin{bmatrix}1.06172&-0.01172\\-0.01172&0.92009\end{bmatrix}$ | $\begin{bmatrix}1.06136&-0.05527\\-0.05527&0.96720\end{bmatrix}$ |
| $\ln\lvert\mathbf C_i\rvert$ | 0.02353 | −0.02322 |
| $\ln P(\omega_i)$ | −0.47000 | −0.98083 |

**Classify $\mathbf x_1=[0.567,1.101]^T$:**

For $\omega_1$: $\mathbf x-\mathbf m_1=[0.541,1.053]^T$, quadratic form $=1.3176$, so $g_1=-\frac12(1.3176)-\frac12(0.02353)-0.47000=-1.1406$.

For $\omega_2$: $\mathbf x-\mathbf m_2=[-0.975,-0.910]^T$, quadratic form $=1.7118$, so $g_2=-\frac12(1.7118)-\frac12(-0.02322)-0.98083=-1.8251$.

$g_1>g_2$ ⇒ **$\mathbf x_1\in\omega_1$** (posterior $\approx0.665$).

**易错点**：忘了先验不等（$\ln P(\omega_i)$ 不能丢）；漏掉 $-\frac12\ln|\mathbf C_i|$（协方差不同不能约去）；二次型交叉项要乘 2。

### 2(b) 朴素贝叶斯决策规则（13 分）

**考点**：Gaussian Naïve Bayes；条件独立假设等价于把协方差矩阵的非对角元置零。

**Answer**

**Assumptions:**

1. **Given the class, the two features are conditionally independent:** $p(\mathbf x|\omega_i)=p(x_1|\omega_i)p(x_2|\omega_i)$.
2. **Each feature is 1-D Gaussian within each class**, mean = component of $\mathbf m_i$, **variance = diagonal element of $\mathbf C_i$** (covariances 0.012, 0.054 ignored).
3. **Priors from sample proportions:** 0.625 and 0.375.

| | $\mu_{i1}$ | $\sigma^2_{i1}$ | $\mu_{i2}$ | $\sigma^2_{i2}$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $\omega_1$ | 0.026 | 0.942 | 0.048 | 1.087 |
| $\omega_2$ | 1.542 | 0.945 | 2.011 | 1.037 |

$$g_i(\mathbf x)=\ln P(\omega_i)+\sum_{k=1}^{2}\Big[-\frac{(x_k-\mu_{ik})^2}{2\sigma_{ik}^2}-\tfrac12\ln\sigma_{ik}^2\Big]$$

Classify $\mathbf x_1=[0.567,1.101]^T$:

- $\omega_1$: $\frac{0.541^2}{0.942}+\frac{1.053^2}{1.087}=1.3308$, $\ln(0.942\times1.087)=0.02367$, $g_1=-0.47000-\frac12(1.3308)-\frac12(0.02367)=-1.1472$.
- $\omega_2$: $\frac{0.975^2}{0.945}+\frac{0.910^2}{1.037}=1.8045$, $\ln(0.945\times1.037)=-0.02024$, $g_2=-0.98083-\frac12(1.8045)-\frac12(-0.02024)=-1.8730$.

$g_1>g_2$ ⇒ **$\mathbf x_1\in\omega_1$** (posterior $\approx0.674$).

**Comparison:** **NB gives almost the same result as full Bayes** ($-1.147$ vs $-1.141$) **because the off-diagonal covariances are small**, i.e. the features are nearly uncorrelated. NB needs only **$2d$ parameters per class** instead of $d+d(d+1)/2$ and **no matrix inversion**.

**【中文】** 假设：①给定类别，两特征**条件独立**；②每个特征类内服从**一维高斯**，均值取 $\mathbf m_i$ 的分量、方差取 $\mathbf C_i$ 的**对角元**；③先验用样本比例。判别函数见上式，结果 $\mathbf x_1$ 判为 $\omega_1$。对比：NB 与完整 Bayes 结果几乎相同，因为协方差非对角元很小、特征近似不相关；NB 每类只需估计 $2d$ 个参数、**不需要矩阵求逆**。

**易错点**：把 NB 做成完整协方差计算；没写 assumptions（题目要求，占分）。

### 3(a) Fisher 线性判别（12 分）

**考点**：$\mathbf w=\mathbf S_W^{-1}(\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2)$，$w_0=-\frac12\mathbf w^T(\mathbf m_1+\mathbf m_2)$。

**Answer**

$$\mathbf S_W=\mathbf S_1+\mathbf S_2=\begin{bmatrix}223.107&-13.506\\-13.506&177.306\end{bmatrix},\quad\det\mathbf S_W=39375.80$$

$$\mathbf S_W^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}0.004503&0.000343\\0.000343&0.005666\end{bmatrix},\quad\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2=\begin{bmatrix}2.444\\-2.595\end{bmatrix}\Rightarrow\mathbf w=\begin{bmatrix}0.010115\\-0.013865\end{bmatrix}$$

$$\mathbf m_1+\mathbf m_2=\begin{bmatrix}-0.718\\-0.593\end{bmatrix},\quad w_0=-\tfrac12[0.010115(-0.718)+(-0.013865)(-0.593)]=-0.00048$$

$$g(\mathbf x)=0.010115x_1-0.013865x_2-0.00048$$

$g>0\Rightarrow\omega_1$; $g<0\Rightarrow\omega_2$.

**Classify $\mathbf x_2=[-0.176,0.224]^T$:** $g=-0.00178-0.00311-0.00048=-0.00537<0$ ⇒ **$\omega_2$**.

Remark: distance to boundary $\lvert g\rvert/\lVert\mathbf w\rVert=0.00537/0.01716=0.313$, versus about 1.768 for the class means — the sample is near the boundary, so confidence is low.

**易错点**：$w_0$ 很小容易误以为算错；$\mathbf m_1-\mathbf m_2$ 第二分量是 $-1.594-1.001=-2.595$，注意符号。

### 4(c) Silhouette coefficient（9 分）

**考点**：内部聚类评价指标的定义、取值范围、含义与用途。

**Answer**

For sample $i$ in cluster $D_p$:

- **$a(i)$: average distance from $i$ to the other samples in the same cluster** — cohesion, smaller is better.
- **$b(i)$: minimum over the other clusters of the average distance from $i$ to that cluster's samples** — separation, larger is better.

$$s(i)=\frac{b(i)-a(i)}{\max\{a(i),b(i)\}},\qquad-1\le s(i)\le1,\qquad S=\frac1N\sum_{i=1}^Ns(i)$$

**Interpretation:** $s\to1$: well clustered; $s\approx0$: on the boundary between two clusters; $s<0$: probably misassigned.

**Use as a criterion:** (1) evaluate a clustering — higher $S$ means tighter, better-separated clusters; (2) **choose $K$** by taking the $K$ with the largest $S$; (3) compare algorithms or parameters; (4) inspect individual $s(i)$ to find boundary points and poor clusters.

**Rationale:** a good clustering has **high intra-cluster similarity and low inter-cluster similarity**; the Silhouette combines both into one normalised quantity bounded in $[-1,1]$, **independent of data scale**. It is an **internal index** — **no ground-truth labels needed**.

**Limitations:** needs all pairwise distances, $O(N^2)$; **favours convex, spherical clusters** and may score arbitrarily shaped clusters (e.g. DBSCAN) low.

**【中文】** 定义：$a(i)$ 是到同簇其他样本的**平均距离**（紧凑性）；$b(i)$ 是到其他各簇平均距离的**最小值**（分离度）；$s(i)=(b-a)/\max(a,b)\in[-1,1]$，整体取平均。含义：接近 1 聚类合理，≈0 在边界上，<0 可能分错簇。用途：评价聚类质量、**选 $K$（取 $S$ 最大）**、比较算法和参数、找边界点和差簇。原理：好的聚类应簇内紧凑、簇间分离，Silhouette 把两者合成一个与数据量纲无关的量；它是**内部指标，不需要真实标签**。局限：计算所有样本对距离，$O(N^2)$；偏好凸的球形簇，对 DBSCAN 的任意形状簇可能给出偏低分数。

**易错点**：$b(i)$ 是到其他簇平均距离的**最小值**；分母是 $\max(a,b)$ 而不是 $a+b$。

## 本卷小结

- 计算量最大：Bayes（2×2 求逆和二次型）、NB、LDA、SVM、两种 linkage，共约 75 分是计算。
- **Bayes + 朴素贝叶斯**是本卷特有的 25 分大题，后几年改成了离散特征的 Bernoulli / Multinomial NB，但高斯情形仍在范围内。先验不等必带 $\ln P(\omega_i)$；协方差不等必带 $-\frac12\ln|\mathbf C_i|$。
- GA 部分考"编码 → 解码 → 适应度"：(a) 优化问题（二进制编码），(b) 建模问题（实数编码、拟合误差）。
